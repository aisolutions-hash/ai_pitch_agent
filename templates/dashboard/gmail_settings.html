How to create a Gmail App Password

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account (myaccount.google.com/security).
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Enter an app name such as KalisoftAI and click Create.
  4. Google shows a 16-character password. Copy it.
  5. Paste it into the App Password field here, then click Save and Test Connection.
Note: your normal Gmail login password will not work. Google requires a special App Password for apps like this one.
